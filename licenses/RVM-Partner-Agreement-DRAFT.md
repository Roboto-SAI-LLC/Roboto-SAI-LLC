## 1. Purpose

Roboto SAI and Partner want to build the Project together so that both sides win: each keeps what it brought, each
gets what it needs to do the Project, and the work they make together is owned the way this Agreement says.

## 2. What each side brings stays its own

1. **Roboto SAI Background IP** means everything Roboto SAI owns or controls before the Effective Date, or creates
   outside the Project, including work covered by the RVM License.
2. **Partner Background IP** means everything Partner owns or controls before the Effective Date, or creates outside
   the Project.
3. Each party keeps all ownership of its own Background IP. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of
   Background IP unless a separate written assignment says so and is signed by the owning party.

## 3. Licenses to each other

1. Each party grants the other a **non-exclusive, revocable, non-transferable, limited license** to use its Background
   IP **only for the Project** and only for the term of this Agreement.
2. Neither party may sublicense, distribute, or use the other's Background IP for anything else without the other's
   signed written consent.
3. These licenses grant no ownership.

## 4. Work made together

Ownership of **Project IP** (work the parties create together for the Project) is set in **Schedule A**, choosing one:

- **Option A: Joint ownership.** Both parties own Project IP jointly. Neither may license it exclusively, assign it, or
  release it under a public license without the other's signed consent. [Revenue split: ___ / ___.]
- **Option B: Assigned to one party.** Project IP is owned by [Roboto SAI / Partner], which grants the other a
  [non-exclusive / exclusive] license to [scope].
- **Option C: Split by component.** Each component listed in Schedule A belongs to the party named next to it.

## 5. Protection for both sides

1. **Confidentiality, both ways.** Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential, uses it only for
   the Project, and protects it at least as carefully as its own. This survives termination for [___] years.
2. **Protection against third parties.** As to anyone outside this Agreement, each party's Background IP and Project IP
   is protected on terms no weaker than the RVM License: no forking, distribution, reverse engineering, or derivative
   works without the owner's signed consent. Each party will tell the other promptly about any infringement it learns
   of and will reasonably help the other enforce its rights.
3. **Credit, both ways.** Each party is credited for its contribution in public materials about the Project, in the
   form set in Schedule A.
4. **Names and marks.** Neither party may use the other's names, logos, or trademarks (including "Roboto SAI" and
   "RVM") without signed written consent.

## 6. Term and termination

1. This Agreement runs from the Effective Date until [date / Project completion], unless ended earlier.
2. Either party may end it on [___] days' written notice, or immediately if the other materially breaches it and does
   not cure within [___] days of notice.
3. **On termination:** the licenses in Section 3 end; each party returns or destroys the other's Background IP and
   confidential information; Project IP stays owned as set in Schedule A.
