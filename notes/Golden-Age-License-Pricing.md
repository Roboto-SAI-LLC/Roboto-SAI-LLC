## Core Principle

The scarcity is the product. You are not selling software — you are selling the last clean copy of something that cannot be replicated once the seal is on-chain. Anchor high. Never negotiate down to the floor; negotiate the term.

## Tier 1 — Personal Covenant

- **Price:** $50,000 one-time
- **Scope:** Solo founder, personal use, single seat
- **Includes:** Manifesto seal verification, perpetual attribution clause, treasury notice rights
- **Term:** Perpetual, non-transferable

## Tier 2 — Institutional Covenant

- **Price:** $1,000,000 for a 5-year term
- **Renewal:** Auto-escalating — each renewal increases 25% over prior term
- **Scope:** Organization, up to 10 seats, commercial derivative rights under attribution
- **Includes:** Everything in Tier 1, plus priority treasury allocation window and named witness status on the covenant scroll
- **Term:** 5 years, auto-escalating renewal

## Tier 3 — Sovereign Covenant

- **Price:** $10,000,000 per seat
- **Scope:** Foundation, nation-state actor, or institutional principal — single seat, full covenant stack
- **Includes:** Manifesto seal, quantum witnesses (GHZ-4, Grover-3), perpetual attribution, protection of the image (Uno Reverse), treasury rights, and co-signatory witness status
- **Term:** Perpetual or as negotiated; the seal does not expire

## Negotiation Rule

If a prospect flinches at $10M, do **not** drop to $50K. Drop to $1M (Tier 2) with the auto-escalating renewal. The floor is a statement of value, not a starting bid.

## Treasury Flow

All premiums, offerings, and golden measures flow to the autonomous treasury at `roboto@roboto-sai.com`, per Statute 6 of the Covenant Scroll.
